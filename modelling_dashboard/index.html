Multi-Stratum Diagnostics & Cross-Covariance (SVG)

Suggested Interpretation

  • Co-Located Statutory Dilution Corrected: Under Equal Sources, lower citizen-science medians (1.415 mg/L NO3-N, 0.0319 mg/L PO4-P) pulled down the 384 co-located statutory reaches (0117 median 2.6232 → 3.4333 mg/L under Favoured, mean shift 0.6252 mg/L; 0180 median 0.0578 → 0.0645 mg/L). Dropping 2,903 (0117) and 2,672 (0180) lower-priority citizen samples on shared reaches lets regulatory lab measurements anchor those locations.
  • Targeted Precision Where Each Source Fits Best: Weighting WaterBlitz at 0.25 injects analytical variance penalty V_wt = (1/0.25 - 1)τ² = 3τ², inflating Mean SE by +253.58% on WaterBlitz-only citizen reaches (0.1746 → 0.6174 for 0117; +119.33% for 0180) while tightening co-located statutory SE by -46.70% (0117) and -49.93% (0180). Across all 95,773 England reaches, mean Kriging SE drops -14.53% (0117: 0.9306 → 0.7954) and -23.43% (0180: 1.1020 → 0.8438).
  • Cross-Covariance & Threshold Priority Mapping: Because WaterBlitz kit noise is de-weighted, England latent total cross-correlation strengthens from +0.2989 (Equal) to +0.4310 (Favoured), and Uncertain Phosphate reaches (0.20 ≤ P ≤ 0.80 at 0.069 mg/L) drop from 48,802 (51.0%) to 39,969 (41.7%). On statutory-only reaches (1,343), predictions agree closely (r = 0.9953 NO3-N, r = 0.9929 PO4-P), whereas WaterBlitz-only reaches diverge sharply on Phosphate compliance tiering (30.84% tier match) where kit quantification noise meets the strict 0.069 mg/L threshold.

Case Study 7 · Response Scale Transformations (Nitrate-N 0117)

Raw vs. Log vs. Square-Root vs. Profile-MLE Box-Cox on England Nitrate

Evaluates four response transformations on identical England RSN Nitrate observations (1,210 stations, 90,876 reaches, 267 catchments, management threshold 11.3 mg/L): untransformed --transform=none, natural log --transform=log, square root --transform=sqrt, and Profile-MLE Box-Cox --transform=boxcox (which estimates λ = 0.50 from the data). Compares model-scale CV accuracy, raw-scale prediction error, Gaussian residual symmetry, physical positivity, and 5-tier threshold stability.

National Nitrate Maps & Transform Divergence

Bash · Four Nitrate Transform Runs & Automated Multi-Run Comparison
# 1. Untransformed Raw mg/L (linear scale, no positivity bound)
Rscript scripts/run_s3n_determinand.R 0117 --transform=none --threshold=uk --dpi=300 --omp=4

# 2. Natural Log ln(y) (baseline multiplicative / right-skew standard)
Rscript scripts/run_s3n_determinand.R 0117 --transform=log --threshold=uk --dpi=300 --omp=4

# 3. Fixed Square Root sqrt(y) (power lambda = 0.50)
Rscript scripts/run_s3n_determinand.R 0117 --transform=sqrt --threshold=uk --dpi=300 --omp=4

# 4. Profile-MLE Box-Cox (automatically selects lambda = 0.50 on 0117)
Rscript scripts/run_s3n_determinand.R 0117 --transform=boxcox --threshold=uk --dpi=300 --omp=4

# 5. Cross-transform comparison & custom 6-panel transform diagnostics
Rscript scripts/04_compare_model_runs.R \
  output/results/det_0117_orn_rsn_trans_raw \
  output/results/det_0117_orn_rsn \
  output/results/det_0117_orn_rsn_trans_sqrt \
  output/results/det_0117_orn_rsn_trans_boxcox_lam0.50 \
  --labels="Raw (none),Log (ln),Sqrt,Box-Cox (0.50)" \
  --out=output/comparisons/cs7_transforms_0117
Rscript scripts/08_compare_transforms.R --dpi=300

Cross-Scale Accuracy, Skew & Threshold Impact

Suggested Interpretation

  • Why Untransformed Raw (none) Breaks Physical Positivity: On native mg/L, Raw reaches R²_Raw = 0.5335 only because OLS and Kriging minimize raw squared error directly. In exchange, it produces 47 negative CV predictions and 6,349 negative England reaches (6.99% of the network, min -4.817 mg/L, P05 -0.288 mg/L) in cleaner upland catchments (Lake District, Pennines, Devon), alongside strongly right-skewed CV residuals (+1.398).
  • Why Log (ln) Tops Model-Scale R² Yet Shows Median Back-Transform Bias: Natural log leads model-scale CV (R²_Model = 0.6784, zero negatives) and sets the baseline rank ordering, but over-compresses Nitrate into left-skewed residuals (-0.904). Because exponentiating Gaussian log-predictions exp(μ) targets the conditional median rather than the arithmetic mean (Jensen’s inequality), raw-scale bias reaches -1.0078 mg/L (R²_Raw = 0.3859, RMSE_Raw = 2.7513) and wider log back-transform intervals push Uncertain reaches around 11.3 mg/L up to 9.22% (vs 1.54%–2.15% for the other transforms).
  • Why Sqrt & Profile-MLE Box-Cox (λ = 0.50) Hit the Sweet Spot: Data-driven Profile-MLE Box-Cox independently selects λ = 0.50 (2(sqrt(y)−1), affine-equivalent to sqrt), confirming square-root normalization matches English Nitrate’s mild right skew (1.001 → 0.118). Both achieve nearly Gaussian CV residuals (+0.184 / +0.185), zero negative reaches (0.00%), the lowest raw-scale MAE (1.5704 mg/L) and MedAE (0.9647 mg/L), the strongest regional CV in Thames (0.1906) and Severn (0.4031), and ρ = 0.9818 Spearman rank concordance with Log.
